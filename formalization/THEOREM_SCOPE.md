- eight distinct indexed coefficient points and their central symmetry;
- outer-conic and dual-caustic algebraic residuals;
- a squared-and-cleared reflection residual;
- all sixteen complete supporting-line incidences with determinant units;

- both exact signed double-areas equal to zero, with both nonzero-denominator

Its axiom audit reported only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`.
