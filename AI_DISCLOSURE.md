# AI disclosure

AI systems assisted candidate discovery, mathematical development, exact
certificate work, adversarial mathematical auditing, literature-search
assistance, implication analysis, and repository staging under human
direction.

Model agreement is not treated as proof, peer review, formal verification, or
evidence of historical priority. The intended public record separates the
mathematical proof, exact certificate, verifier replay, literature audit,
formalization status, and human release decisions.

No AI system is proposed as an author. The mathematical article follows the
project's established authorless-PDF convention; repository and
preferred-citation authorship use `DannyExperiments`, with no affiliation
listed.

Raw model transcripts, private receipts, personal screenshots, local absolute
paths, and internal tool metadata are excluded from this tree.
