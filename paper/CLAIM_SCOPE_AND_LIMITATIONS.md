# Claim scope and limitations

## Exact public claim

Under the complete perpendicular supporting-line antipedal construction,
there is an exact primitive counterclockwise one-winding `N=8`
elliptic-billiard orbit in a noncircular ellipse, tangent on every open side
segment to one nondegenerate confocal elliptic caustic, for which all sixteen
focal antipedal intersections are finite and both focal signed shoelace areas
are zero. Therefore the unqualified ordinary quotient in
arXiv:2004.12497v11 source-table row `k_607` is undefined `0/0` at that phase.

The signed-area equality survives. For a centrally symmetric ordered even
polygon with opposite reference points and finite complete supporting-line
intersections, central inversion maps the two antipedal polygons by a cyclic
shift and preserves signed area. In the established simple one-winding
even elliptic-caustic billiard scope, this gives equality of the two focal
signed areas and quotient value `1` on the common nonzero locus.

## Priority language

The exact primitive focal `N=8` simultaneous-zero certificate appears new
after a documented three-lane search through 2026-08-12. This is a bounded
negative-search result with moderate-high confidence, not a proof of absolute
or first-ever priority.

No novelty claim attaches to:

- complete-line antipedal geometry;
- signed polygonal area;
- central symmetry of the relevant simple even elliptic billiards;
- focus exchange by central inversion;
- the resulting all-even equality mechanism;
- restricting an ordinary quotient to a nonzero denominator;
- zero antipedal areas in general.

## Mandatory exclusions

The manuscript does not claim:

- a literal single-half-ray theorem or counterexample;
- that the two focal signed areas are unequal;
- a defined quotient value different from `1`;
- a canonical removable value for `0/0`;
- a result about the journal item numbered `k607`, which is a different pedal
  formula;
- a result about arXiv-v11 `k_606` or neighboring rows;
- zero existence for any period other than the exact `N=8` witness;
- a whole-family `N=8` zero theorem;
- odd-period, unsigned-area, other-caustic, or arbitrary-Poncelet extensions;
- a classification, dimension, stability, smoothness, or uniqueness theorem
  for the common-zero locus;
- three independent witnesses, a second solve, or a second open problem;
- peer review, absolute novelty, or full formal verification.

## Verification boundary

The human-readable proof carries root isolation, real geometry, cyclic order,
open-segment tangency, reflection-sign control, finite intersections, exact
area factorization, source semantics, and partial-division logic.

The standard-library verifier corroborates the finite exact algebra. A partial
