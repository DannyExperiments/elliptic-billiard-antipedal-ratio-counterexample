# Scope and limitations

## Included

- arXiv:2004.12497v11 source-table row `k_607`;
- complete perpendicular supporting lines;
- cyclic signed polygonal area;
- an exact `N=8` admissible common-zero certificate; and
- the corrected pointwise identity for exact even orbits with finite
  intersections.

## Excluded

- literal oriented half-rays: the certified witness does not form the needed
  cyclic polygon under a single-half-ray reading;
- unsigned area or any absolute-area functional;
- a defined quotient different from `1`;
- common-zero existence at any other period or caustic;
- a classification, uniqueness, multiplicity, stability, or dimension result
  for the zero locus;
- odd periods, other Poncelet pairs, or nonconfocal systems;
- any neighboring arXiv row;
- the differently numbered formula in the 2021 journal article; and
- any second named open problem.

The phrase “apparently new” may be used only for the exact `N=8`
simultaneous-zero certificate and only together with the documented search
cutoff `2026-08-12`. It is not an absolute-priority claim.
