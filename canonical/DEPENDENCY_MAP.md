# Dependency map

| Claim | Required inputs | Does not require |
|---|---|---|
| Half-turn maps the first focal supporting lines to the second | `P_(i+N/2)=-P_i`, opposite foci, complete-line convention | The `N=8` quartic |
| Ordered antipedal polygons are congruent | Line equivariance and finite consecutive intersections | Area computation |
| Focal cyclic signed areas agree | Congruence by an orientation-preserving half-turn and cyclic shift | Nonzero area |
| Quotient equals `1` where defined | Signed-area equality and common area nonzero | The exact witness |
| Common focal zero sets coincide in the audited all-even domain | Pointwise signed-area equality | Existence of a zero |
| One admissible common zero exists | Full exact `N=8` billiard and area certificate | A general zero-locus theorem |
| The source quotient is not total | Exact admissible common zero and ordinary real division | A nonunit quotient value |

## Load-bearing witness checks

The public certificate must independently establish all of the following:

1. the root exists uniquely in the stated rational interval;
2. the ellipse and confocal caustic are nondegenerate;
3. the eight vertices are distinct, cyclically ordered, primitive, and
   one-winding;
4. every side is tangent to the same caustic on its open segment;
5. the billiard reflection law holds;
6. all sixteen supporting-line intersections are finite; and
7. both cyclic signed areas vanish exactly.

Failure of any one item blocks the domain-counterexample claim.
