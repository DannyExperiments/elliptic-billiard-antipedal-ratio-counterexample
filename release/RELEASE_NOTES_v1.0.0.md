## Result

inversion identifies the two cyclic signed areas for every exact even orbit
with finite consecutive intersections. Their quotient is therefore `1`
exactly on the common nonzero locus.

The exact primitive one-winding `N=8` certificate has all sixteen
supporting-line intersections finite but makes both signed areas zero. Thus

expression `0/0` at an admissible phase. This is a domain counterexample, not
a defined nonunit ratio and not a refutation of the signed-area identity.
